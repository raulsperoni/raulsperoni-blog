🚴 Strava OAuth Setup

This tool helps you get a Strava refresh token with the activity:read_all scope for your static blog.

Step 1: Enter Your Strava App Credentials

Get these from Strava API Settings

Step 2: Authorize the App

Click the authorization URL above, log in to Strava, and click "Authorize".

You'll be redirected to a URL like: http://localhost/?state=&code=XXXXX&scope=...

The page won't load (that's OK!), just copy the entire URL from your browser's address bar.
